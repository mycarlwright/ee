Civil Engineer

Opportunity for a project running Senior Civil Engineer, Chartered, near qualified or Incorporated Engineer to join an award winning multi-disciplinary property and construction consultancy in Cambridge.

The role

A team providing a fully integrated design and technical expertise working on commercial, education, defence, residential and health projects at every stage of development. This is a key role where you’ll be instrumental in the commercial and technical success of civil engineering projects and help shape the team. Working across all sectors, from building drainage to civils focused infrastructure, helping provide a professional civil engineering service to the practice and its clients.

As Civil Engineer, you will work with a multi-disciplinary team to design practical civil solutions from concept to delivery, dealing with deal with client briefs, cost plans, project meetings, design, technical advice and financial input into projects and bids. Experience working with a mix of public and private work would be an advantage.

What's on offer

In return for your broad range of civil engineering design, building regulation knowledge and UK consultancy experience the position offers a competitive salary up to £60,000, health benefits and hybrid working 2-3 days in the office each week.

Apply with your CV or call the team to find out more about this supportive and employee-focused consultancy and discuss your career preferences with us.
