Civil & Structural Engineering

Structural Engineer

Join an award winning multi-disciplinary property and construction consultancy in Cambridge and help shape the structural engineering service, be part of a collaborative team with real project variety.

The role

Work on commercial, education, defence, residential and health projects at every stage of development. The team provides technical expertise and in-house multi-disciplinary support to clients and project partners nationwide. Help promote the structural service, grow fee income and design innovative and practical structures aligned with architectural and building concepts.

As a Senior Structural Engineer on the team, you will lead detailed and concept designs and assist with supervising projects, establishing client breifs, technical requirements and scope of works, tender preparation, project delivery, bid proposals and fee growth. You will help build and develop client relationships and be involved in project and technical resource requirements. Experience working across a broad of materials and UK project sectors including education, healthcare and local authority sectors is essential. You'll need proficiency using BIM, 3D design tools, Revit and a good technical understanding of structural standards and building regulations.

What's on offer

In return for your communication, problem solving and post qualification structural design experience, the practice offers a competitive salary up to £60,000, health benefits, team events, hybrid remote working with a balance of office and home days and professional development.

Apply with your CV or call the team to find out more about this supportive and employee-focused practice and discuss your career preferences with us.
