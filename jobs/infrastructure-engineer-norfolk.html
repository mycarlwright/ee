Civil Design Engineer

Civil Engineer with an infrastructure design background proficient with section agreements and drainage design. Join a Norfolk based multi-disciplinary team, bringing consultants, architects, and civil & structural engineers together to deliver award-winning projects.

The role

An experienced Civil Engineer or Infrastructure Design Engineer providing detailed design, site and coordination support, you'll be involved in drainage and pavement designs on a wide variety of civil and infrastructure projects.

This is not just a design role you'll enjoy project coordination and work closely with PMs, Surveyors and contractor teams to deliver schemes at all stages.

It is a hands-on role where you will also contribute to project management plans, prepare reports and visit sites to report on technical feasibility and design compliance.

What's on offer

Join a team where your judgement is trusted, your experience is valued and you'll be encouraged to help mentor others. A genuinely varied project portfolio from education, defence, retail, commercial, medical and research facilities through to award-winning residential, decarbonisation and civil engineering projects. Salary £50-50k + car + gym benefits to reflect your breadth of project experience.

Company car or car allowance alternative, competitive holiday and professional memberships paid.

About you

  • Working knowledge of key standards and design guides, DMRB, County Guides
  • Experience deailing with S104, S106 and S38 agreements
  • Based locally in Norfolk and a willingness to work across the region
  • Proficient in using Causeway Flow / Microdrainage and knowledge of Civil 3D benefical
