Structural Engineer

Opportunity for a Structural Engineer working towards chartered status to join an award winning multi-disciplinary property and construction consultancy in Oxford.

The role

A team providing a fully integrated design and technical expertise working on commercial, education, defence, residential and health projects at every stage of development. Help design practical and economical structural solutions working aligned with architectural concepts. Delivering coordinated designs collaborating with a wider project team, helping provide a professional building structures service to the practice and its clients.

As Structural Engineer, you will work with a multi-disciplinary team undetaking structural surveys and producing clear technical reports, design details and contribute to bid proposals and fee quotations. You will help build and maintain strong client relationships and help from concept to delivery. Experience working with a range of materials and project sectors including new build development, refurbishment and alterations is essential. Proficiency using BIM and 3D design tools, Revit and a good technical understanding of structural standards and regulations.

What's on offer

In return for your structural design experience, building regulation knowledge and UK consultancy experience the position offers a competitive salary up to £45,000, health benefits and hybrid working 2-3 days in the office each week.

Apply with your CV or call the team to find out more about this supportive and employee-focused consultancy and discuss your career preferences with us.
