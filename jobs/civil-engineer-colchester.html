Civil & Structural Engineering

Civil Engineer

Are you a Civil Engineer with UK residential development and infrastructure experience? Join a growing consultant known for delivering innovative solutions and forward-thinking designs and be a key member of our supportive and independent team

The role

A business known for industry and environmental best practice, you’ll help drive projects that prioritise innovation, safety, and sustainable infrastructure development.

You will also work on drainage, transport planning and feasibility work to help shape pre-development stages and have the chance to broaden your exposure with street lighting design.

The team provides a full in-house design package, including civil infrastructure, structural engineering, utility design management, civil groundworks, land surveying and site engineering services. Working from a rural office location which avoids the busy commute into the surrounding towns.

Working at the office together to collaborate on projects in person, the team has a real focus on training and development. As the Senior Engineer, you’ll manage your own clients and projects from resource to completion, preparing drainage strategy reports to support planning applications and detailed design.

You will liaise with local authorities and statutory bodies, conduct site visits and consider H&S and risk.

As Civil Engineer you will be skills in section agreement submissions, private drainage and external works design, AutoCAD, Civils 3D and hydraulic modelling. HND or degree in civil engineering.

What's on offer

Competitive salary to reflect experience, generous holiday and paid professional memberships. Locally based in Essex or East Anglia and willing to be office based and travel to sites is essential.
