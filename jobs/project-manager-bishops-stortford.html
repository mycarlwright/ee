Civil & Structural Engineering

Project Manager

A rare opportunity for a high-level Project Manager with client side refurbishment project experience, to join an innovative building and site investigation consultancy in East Anglia.

The role

You will be working with a team specialising in lifecycle building remediation, bespoke geophysical testing and site investigation of historical buildings, infrastructure and commercial projects. This is a key delivery role for a Project Manager to take a high-level overview of programme and client management on projects across the business.

Work from either a Hertfordshire or Cambridgeshire based office with one day a week travelling to visit UK wide projects.

Lead the planning, coordination and delivery of technically complex projects, ensuring they are delivered safely, efficiently, on programme and within budget. Liaising with clients throughout the lifecycle of each project, building strong relationships that support partnerships and repeat business, while keeping clients and stakeholders informed of progress, key findings, risks and delivery dates.

You will also support the preparation of project proposals, programmes and commercial estimates, working closely with technical and commercial colleagues, and be involved in scoping activities including site visits and client meetings. Day to day, you'll coordinate internal technical teams, subcontractors and external stakeholders, monitor budgets, resources and programme, and manage the reporting, documentation and quality assurance that keeps projects on track.

There's a leadership element to - mentoring junior team members, reviewing projects for continuous improvement and promoting collaborative working.

What's on offer

This is an excellent opportunity for an experienced Project Manager to step into a senior, client-facing role with genuine autonomy and real influence over how projects and processes are delivered as the business scales

You'll be joining a well-established, independent and growing technical consultancy with an outstanding reputation, unique projects and a diverse and international client base. No two projects are the same, and you'll have genuine ownership of clients, along with the opportunity to shape internal project management practices as the company grows. Work from either a Herts or Cambs based office with one day a week travelling to visit UK wide projects.

To apply

You'll need broad, demonstrable experience managing refurbishment projects in relation to the existing building structure, building durability and extending lifecycles. Experience commissioning or managing a range of surveys or investigation consultants and contractors is highly desirable. Strong client and stakeholder management skills are essential, along with experience managing budgets, schedules, risks and resources. APM, PRINCE2, PMP or equivalent and a technical background such as MCIOB, MRICS or similar is beneficial but not essential.

Apply with your CV to be shortlisted or call to discuss your refurbishment experience in confidence.
